## How it was written

The general method is in [../METHOD.md](../METHOD.md): which controls are chosen, how a control is matched to scanner findings or left to a person, and what the severity floors and evidence windows mean. For this framework:

- SOC 2 is audited by a CPA against criteria the organisation interprets, so this framework shows evidence an auditor may ask for, not a result.
- CC7.1 (detecting new vulnerabilities) reads recent scans and open high or critical findings; CC6.1 reads secrets in code as a logical-access weakness; CC6.8 reads supply-chain findings as malicious software entering through the build.
- Evidence window: **30 days.** A scan older than this does not evidence a control.
- 7 controls: 4 matched to scanner findings, 3 manual.

## What it does not cover

- The criteria on availability, confidentiality, processing integrity and privacy are not listed.
- Most common criteria (control environment, communication, risk assessment, monitoring) are organisational and are not listed.
- The AICPA numbering is under revision (docs/frameworks/README.md of this repository will note it when it changes).

## How to read the result

A matched control reads `clear` when a scan that could evidence it ran within the window and nothing it counts is open, `open` when something is open, and `not assessed` when no such scan ran. A manual control stays unassessed until a person attests it. A `clear` control means *no open finding of the kind the control watches*; it does not prove the requirement is met.
