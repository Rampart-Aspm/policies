## How it was written

The general method is in [../METHOD.md](../METHOD.md): which controls are chosen, how a control is matched to scanner findings or left to a person, and what the severity floors and evidence windows mean. For this framework:

- Unlike the other frameworks, the weakness lists here are not our approximation: they are the references OWASP publishes on each risk's page, in the order OWASP gives them, and nothing was added or removed except as the note says.
- A weakness-only control is evidenced by a code scan (`sast` or `dast`), the server's rule; for APIs a dynamic scan is the usual evidence.
- Evidence window: **30 days.** A scan older than this does not evidence a control.
- 10 controls: 9 matched to scanner findings, 1 manual.

## What it does not cover

- API6 (sensitive business flows) is about abuse of legitimate functionality, which no scanner reads; OWASP lists no weakness for it.
- CWE-2, CWE-16 and CWE-388 are groupings, not weaknesses; a scanner that reports them is rare, so API8 is mostly read through the other weaknesses it lists.

## How to read the result

A matched control reads `clear` when a scan that could evidence it ran within the window and nothing it counts is open, `open` when something is open, and `not assessed` when no such scan ran. A manual control stays unassessed until a person attests it. A `clear` control means *no open finding of the kind the control watches*; it does not prove the requirement is met.
