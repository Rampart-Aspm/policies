# NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 3: protecting controlled unclassified information

_Generated by `scripts/generate.py` from `frameworks/nist-800-171-r3.yaml`. Do not edit this file._

The requirements for protecting federal controlled unclassified information in non-federal systems, which US defence contractors are assessed against, restricted to those that scanning can speak to.

## Where it comes from

- **Resource:** `nist-800-171-r3` (a `ComplianceFramework`), switched on with `compliance.nist800171.enabled`. It reads only applications that list `nist-800-171` in `complianceScope`.
- **Version covered:** Rev. 3
- **Source:** <https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/171/r3/final>
- **Logo:** <https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Rampart-Aspm/policies/main/logos/nist-800-171-r3.svg>, a mark Rampart draws itself (a flat shape with the framework's letters), not the publisher's logo; see [../METHOD.md](../METHOD.md), "Logos".
- **Verification: primary.** Identifiers and titles were read from the publisher's own text or machine-readable data.
  - How: Every id and title was checked against NIST's OSCAL catalogue of Rev. 3 (usnistgov/oscal-content, SP 800-171 Rev. 3 JSON). All 15 are present with exactly the titles shown. Only Rev. 3 is published in OSCAL, which is why Rev. 2 is not offered.
- **Licence and wording:** NIST publications are in the public domain, so the published titles are used as they are.

## How it was written

The general method is in [../METHOD.md](../METHOD.md): which controls are chosen, how a control is matched to scanner findings or left to a person, and what the severity floors and evidence windows mean. For this framework:

- The same selection principle as for SP 800-53 (METHOD.md): risk and vulnerability management, configuration, cryptography, authenticators, flaw and malicious-code handling, and supply chain.
- 03.11.02 and 03.14.01 read open findings of severity high or above, as RA-5 and SI-2 do in SP 800-53, of which this publication is a tailored subset.
- 03.13.08 joins transmission and storage in one requirement, so it carries both weakness lists.
- Evidence window: **30 days.** The vulnerability-scanning requirement asks for periodic scans; monthly is the common practice.
- 15 controls: 7 matched to scanner findings, 8 manual.

## What it does not cover

- Rev. 2 numbering (for example 3.11.2) is not covered. Use a Rev. 2 mapping if the assessment is against Rev. 2.
- No control measures time (docs/FOUNDATION.md, item 2).

## How to read the result

A matched control reads `clear` when a scan that could evidence it ran within the window and nothing it counts is open, `open` when something is open, and `not assessed` when no such scan ran. A manual control stays unassessed until a person attests it. A `clear` control means *no open finding of the kind the control watches*; it does not prove the requirement is met.
