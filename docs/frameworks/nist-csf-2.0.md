## How it was written

The general method is in [../METHOD.md](../METHOD.md): which controls are chosen, how a control is matched to scanner findings or left to a person, and what the severity floors and evidence windows mean. For this framework:

- Subcategories were chosen where software, vulnerabilities, configuration or the supply chain are the subject: GV.SC (supply chain), ID.AM-02 (software inventory), ID.RA (vulnerability and risk identification), ID.IM-02, PR.PS (platform security), PR.DS (data security) and PR.AA-01 (credentials).
- The framework is outcome-based. 'Vulnerabilities in assets are identified, validated, and recorded' (ID.RA-01) is a statement about a process; here it reads as: scanned recently, and nothing critical left open. That is a deliberate, weaker reading, named as such in each control.
- DE.CM-09 (monitoring of runtime environments) is not listed: it concerns runtime monitoring, which no scanner Rampart reads provides.
- Evidence window: **30 days.** Continuous identification of vulnerabilities is the outcome; monthly is the common practice.
- 15 controls: 8 matched to scanner findings, 7 manual.

## How to read the result

A matched control reads `clear` when a scan that could evidence it ran within the window and nothing it counts is open, `open` when something is open, and `not assessed` when no such scan ran. A manual control stays unassessed until a person attests it. A `clear` control means *no open finding of the kind the control watches*; it does not prove the requirement is met.
